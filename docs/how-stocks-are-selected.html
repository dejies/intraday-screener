1

The stock list when the app starts

The screener starts from watchlist.csv, every stock listed on NSE (3,226). It drops the 13 that Dhan doesn't recognise and gets live prices for the remaining 3,213 from Dhan's feed.

2

Yesterday's numbers once a day, at start

For each stock it loads about a year of daily prices and works out:

  • yesterday's close, high and low, and the 52-week high and low;
  • how much it usually moves in a day (the ATR %, averaged over 14 days);
  • how much money it usually trades (turnover, averaged over 20 days).

A stock with less than two days of history (a brand-new listing) waits here.

3

Hard filters re-checked every 2 seconds

A stock must pass all of these. If it fails one, it goes to Neutral & filtered with the reason, and stops here.

RuleYour setting
It's a normal "EQ" share (not trade-for-trade or a small-company SME share)EQ only
It has enough history to know its typical daily movement14 days
It usually moves at least this much in a day1.5%
Yesterday's closing price is at most₹1,000
It usually trades at least this much money a dayoff

The price limit uses yesterday's close, so the list stays fixed all day: a ₹990 stock that rallies to ₹1,050 stays in.

4

Full indicator scan every 5 minutes

Of the stocks that passed, the 500 most traded get 5-minute charts and every indicator: MACD, RSI, ADX, the moving averages (EMAs), VWAP and today's relative volume (RVOL).

Only finished 5-minute candles are used, so the numbers don't jump around while a candle is still forming. Stocks outside the 500 still get live prices, but not the strategy checks.

5

Votes → Bullish, Bearish or Neutral every 2 seconds

Seven yes/no questions. Each one votes "up", "down", or not at all:

  1. Is the price above or below the day's average traded price (VWAP)?
  2. Is MACD above or below its signal line?
  3. Are buyers or sellers pushing harder (+DI vs −DI)?
  4. Is the stock beating or trailing the NIFTY 50 by at least 0.2%?
  5. Has the price broken above the first 15 minutes' high, or below their low?
  6. Are the 5-minute moving averages lined up upward or downward?
  7. Are the daily moving averages lined up upward or downward?
4 or more "up" votes, and more up than down → Bullish. The same rule with "down" votes → Bearish. Anything else → Neutral.

Two bonus points measure strength: a real trend (ADX ≥ 20) and above-normal volume today (Day RVOL ≥ 1.5×). Votes plus bonus points give the score out of 9 on the Bullish / Bearish tab.

6

Strategy checks → MOM, BRK, LDR every 2 seconds

Every stock from step 4 is checked against three strategies. It qualifies for a strategy only if every one of that strategy's checks passes. One "no" is enough to miss.

MOMStrong Momentum

Already rising strongly, with its sector behind it.

  • Volume today over 2.5× normal
  • Its sector is beating NIFTY
  • The stock is beating its sector
  • MACD above zero and still growing
  • Price above VWAP, and holding there
  • RSI above 50
  • A real uptrend (ADX above 20, buyers stronger)

BRKEarly Breakout

Just broke a key price level on heavy volume.

  • Volume today over 2× normal
  • In the last 30 minutes, a 5-min candle closed above the first-15-min high, yesterday's high, or the top of a tight 1-hour range
  • That candle had over 2× the usual volume for that time of day
  • RSI above 50, or rising
  • MACD turning up or strengthening
  • ADX rising

LDRSector Leaders

The best stock in a strong sector.

  • Its sector is beating NIFTY
  • The stock is beating its sector
  • In the top 10% of its sector today
  • Volume today over 2.5× normal
  • Price above VWAP
  • Short-term uptrend (EMA 9 above EMA 20)
All three look for stocks going up. On a falling day, few qualify. On 25 Nov, the check that stopped most stocks was "volume over 2.5× normal" (187 of 201 stocks checked).
7

Setup score, 0 to 100 with each check

Each candidate gets a score to rank it against the others:

80+ is a "strong candidate", 65+ "good", 50+ "watchlist", below that "low priority". It's a way to rank candidates, not a chance of making money.

8

Joining and leaving the candidate list continuous

qualifies→ 30 s → NEW (10 min)→ active→ stops qualifying → weakening→ 10 min → removed
  • A stock must keep qualifying for 30 seconds before it's listed, so a one-off blip doesn't count.
  • If it stops qualifying, it's marked weakening. If it qualifies again within 10 minutes, it stays.
  • Otherwise it's removed, and stays greyed out for 30 minutes so you can see that it dropped.
9

Alerts when something changes

An alert fires when a stock qualifies or drops out, breaks a level (the first-15-min high or low, yesterday's high or low, the 52-week high or low), or has a sudden volume spike on a 1-minute candle. Stocks above your ₹1,000 limit get no alerts.

10

What you choose to see your dashboard

The filter bar (sector, strategy, minimum score, Day RVOL, Chg % from–to, direction) only hides or shows rows. It never changes steps 1–9 or the alerts.

Example: one stock, start to finish

Take a ₹290 stock that usually moves 5% a day and is up 9% today:

  1. It's an EQ share, closed under ₹1,000 and usually moves more than 1.5%, so it passes the filters (step 3).
  2. If it's among the 500 most traded, it gets the full scan (step 4).
  3. Price above VWAP, MACD up, beating NIFTY: it's probably Bullish (step 5).
  4. For Strong Momentum it also needs volume over 2.5× normal and a sector beating NIFTY (step 6). If either is missing, it isn't a candidate, even with a 9% move.
  5. Click why? on its row in the dashboard to see each check with ✓ or ✗ and the real numbers.

Where to change these

Every number here can be changed on the dashboard's Settings page (most take effect straight away). Your changes are saved in data/settings.json; the defaults are in config.py. The full formulas are in technical_indicators_calculations.md.
